# FAQ page

## Fix: Wi-Fi / kitchen (it currently repeats the parking answer)
**Is there Wi-Fi and a full kitchen?**
Yes. Every home has working Wi-Fi and a full kitchen with the cookware and basics to actually cook. The Wi-Fi name and password are in your arrival instructions.

## Update: Check-in / check-out
**What are check-in and check-out times?**
Most homes are 4:00 PM check-in and 11:00 AM check-out. Go by the times in your confirmation, since a few homes are different.

## New answers
**Is my booking held while I check out?**
No. Your dates aren't held until checkout is complete and you get a confirmation. If someone else books first, the dates will show as unavailable.

**What does the total include?**
The total at checkout includes the nightly rate, the cleaning fee and applicable taxes. If you're bringing a pet, the pet fee is collected separately after you book.

**Do you offer weekly or monthly discounts?**
Yes, and they apply automatically: 10% off stays of 7 nights or more and 20% off stays of 28 nights or more. No code needed.

**Can I stay a month or longer?**
Yes. We host a lot of travel nurses, military families and people relocating. For month-to-month stays, see our [Monthly & Extended Stays](/month-to-month-furnished-rentals/) page to request a quote.

**Are there quiet hours?**
Quiet hours are 10 PM to 8 AM. That means no loud music, parties or yelling. Normal living sounds like talking, cooking and walking around are fine.

**Can I check in early?**
Early check-in is free when the home is empty the night before and there's no same-day turnover. Message us a day or two ahead and we'll let you know.

**Do the Washington homes have air conditioning?**
Our Washington homes don't have central AC, which is common in the area. We provide portable AC units and fans as a courtesy for warm days.
